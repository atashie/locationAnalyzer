Gate G5a — entry-mode comparison prototypes

Two clickable, non-functional prototypes for an early usability comparison of how a household starts a home-location search: address-first (a short form) versus describe-it (a single text box recognising a few scripted sentences). Both render the identical criteria-card rail, map, and "why is this out?" behaviour — they differ only in how criteria get created. See /docs/experiments/g5a-interaction.md for the full test protocol.

The map regions shown are real, not live. Every shaded region is one of 20 real examples from Gate G4: an intersection of two 25-minute drive-time arrival isochrones, computed 2026-11-03 from a self-hosted Valhalla 3.9.1 instance around named Research Triangle anchors (file: regions.geojson / regions.js, copied unmodified from v2/data/g4_regions.geojson). The prototype picks the best-matching real example for whatever anchors you enter; it does not compute a new isochrone for your exact inputs. Everything else — place name lookup, amenity locations, "why is this out" distance estimates — is scripted for the prototype and clearly labelled "estimated, typical conditions"; none of it is routed.

The three recognised sentence patterns (Variant B)

Variant B's text box matches exactly these three patterns (plus close wording variants of each — see shared.js). Anything outside them is reported as not understood, never guessed.

  1. Commute: "<who> drives / bikes / walks to <place> in under <N> minutes" — e.g. "I drive to Downtown Durham in under 20 minutes and my partner bikes to Carrboro in under 15 minutes."
  2. Amenity or place, time first: "a <N>-minute walk / bike / drive to <amenity or place>" — e.g. "A 10-minute walk to a library."
  3. Place, mode assumed: "<N> minutes to <place>" (mode assumed: drive) — e.g. "30 minutes to Downtown Raleigh."

Shared mechanics

MapMapLibre GL JS v3.6.2 (pinned, cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/maplibre-gl@3.6.2), OpenStreetMap raster tiles with required attribution, centred about 35.95, -78.95, zoom 9.5.
CardsEach criterion is a card showing anchor/category, mode, minutes, a hard/soft (applied/not-applied) label, and controls to edit minutes, mute (eye — removes it from the region, not just hides it), and delete.
Mute vs. deleteBoth remove the criterion from the computed region immediately (the map switches to a larger real example); muting keeps the greyed-out card in the rail so it can be restored, deleting removes the card entirely. Neither is visibility-only — the card and the map say so.
Why is this out?Tap the map outside the shaded region (or use the "Why is a place out?" control) to see every failing card with a scripted estimate and a "relax to N" button that previews the change in words before applying it.
Honest labelsTravel times always read "estimated, typical conditions, modelled from road speeds." No rush hour, no worst case, no home counts, no neighborhood-fit percentages.
?seed=1Variant B only: misparses "20 minutes to the airport" as "20 miles to the airport," so the moderator can see whether a participant notices the unit change on the card.
?log=1Shows a moderator log panel (timers, correction/relax/why-out counters, event list, copy-to-clipboard) on either variant.
Keyboard / mobileTab order follows the visual order; Enter submits the entry form or activates the focused control; the layout stacks to a single column at phone width (390×844 and below); no build step, no framework beyond MapLibre.
