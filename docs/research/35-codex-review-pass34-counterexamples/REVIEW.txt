(g) HIGH: rebuild recommendation is directionally right but incomplete; penalty
recommendation describes an engineering proposal, not an available class knob.
Sources: stale-raster lines 68-81; soft-way lines 92-97.

Use a coherent surface build: seconds .npy, reason .npy, min_possible_s .npy,
_build.json with their digests and parameters, encoded .tif derived from stored
float32 seconds; then verify and regenerate the appropriate reports. The S2
builder, spikes/s2_building_weighted_coverage.py grocery walk, writes that bundle.
The recommended G1 script writes seconds/TIFF/oneway mask but not reasons,
bounds or build sidecar; S0 verifies/reports but does not write those arrays.
The present symmetrise-only change preserves finiteness; this observation is not
a general substitute for regenerating and binding the companion artifacts.

Regenerate data/s0_status_triangle_grocery_walk.json AFTER the new layer and check
without --allow-artifact-mismatch. Refresh graph-derived one-way masks if consumed.
Regenerate audit draws, their .meta.json raster hashes/census, and destination
enrichment against that new build. Re-running recover_audit_destinations alone
does not regenerate samples or their metadata. Preserve field observations by
stable cell/journey identity and version the replacement sheets rather than
overwriting open files. Recompute affected measurement outputs, figures, and
claims that read the raster (including verify_e1c_figures.py's figures).

The frozen B/C sample frames themselves do NOT depend on travel time: B depends
on buildings/grid/seed, C on grid/seed. Preserve them; recompute their layer-based
measurements. The frames' manifests and build_dev_frames.py confirm those inputs.

The CURRENT browser manifest/bins do not consume this Triangle raster. The
exporter's filename regex rejects own_triangle_grocery_walk.tif; discovery
selects data/layers/own_grocery_walk.tif instead. Running the exporter after a
Triangle rebuild will therefore not publish the corrected Triangle layer. Decide
the intended browser dataset first; change discovery/configuration and regenerate
both bins and manifest together if Triangle is to be served. A ceil encoding
stamp identifies rounding, not the graph revision or source-raster identity.

access_penalty_s consults access/foot/etc tags, not highway classes. Open track,
path, footway and service examples all return zero. The two-weight downstream
machinery can support a new class penalty, so penalisation IS a real engineering
option; an explicit class rule, value and measurement remain to be implemented.
Moreover, finite penalties do NOT guarantee losing to any paved alternative:
our executable 100 s track + 120 s penalty beats a 1,000 s paved road, reporting
100 s. Guaranteed preference requires a different ordering/policy. The walked
route already pays private-access penalties on its track segments and still wins.
Do not conflate class, surface, permission, and verified traversability.

profile_version currently hashes constants and walk_symmetrised=True, not the
symmetrise implementation: replacing the function leaves its hash unchanged
(speeds-v0+9893b1b0168ff19a). Existing profile keys cannot invalidate this change.

Executable evidence: analyse.py -> browser_sources; check_policy.py;
