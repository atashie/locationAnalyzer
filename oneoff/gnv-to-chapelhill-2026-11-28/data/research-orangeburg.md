# Research: Orangeburg SC (+ Santee)

Position: **Sat 4:56 / arrive 15:19 / Sun 4:04 via I-95 or 4:19 inland / detour +0:41 / frac 0.55.**
Arrives with **~2 hours of daylight — 42 minutes more than Columbia**, the most of any
recommended stop. And it is **8 minutes further inside the 45-minute detour budget** than
Columbia.

See `../analysis.md` §3 follow-up: **Orangeburg can avoid I-95 entirely for only +15 minutes**
(I-26 → I-77 → I-85 → I-40, 4:19), which was the main objection to it in the first pass.

## The headline problem: no indoor pool can be confirmed in Orangeburg

This is the finding that should decide how you treat Orangeburg. All its interstate hotels sit
in one cluster at **I-26 Exit 145 (US-601)**, on Citadel Rd / St Matthews Rd, 8–10 minutes from
Edisto Memorial Gardens. **Sources flatly contradict each other on every "indoor pool" claim:**

| Property | hotelguides | reservations.com | Booking.com | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Holiday Inn Express & Suites**, 118 Sleep Inn Rd | "Heated indoor" | **"Seasonal outdoor"** | silent | **contested 1–1** |
| **Comfort Inn & Suites**, 746 Citadel Rd | "Heated indoor" | city list "seasonal outdoor"; property page "an indoor pool" | silent | **source contradicts itself** |
| **SpringHill Suites**, 675 Citadel Rd | "Heated indoor" | not listed | silent | **single source only** |
| **Spark by Hilton**, 731 Citadel Rd | "Heated indoor" *(as Country Inn)* | **"Seasonal outdoor"** *(as Spark)* | **"outdoor"** | **legacy data — see below** |
| Fairfield Inn, 663 Citadel Rd | outdoor/summer | seasonal outdoor | silent | outdoor |
| **Hampton Inn & Suites**, 749 Citadel Rd | outdoor/summer | outdoor, "no indoor pool mentioned" | "seasonal outdoor" | **outdoor, triple-confirmed** |
| Tru by Hilton, 739 Citadel Rd | "Pool: None" | not listed | fitness only | **no pool** |

**The Country Inn → Spark by Hilton rebrand is a real trap.** The old reservations.com slug for
"Country Inn & Suites Orangeburg" now resolves to **Spark by Hilton, 731 Citadel Road, 81
guestrooms** — same address, same room count as the hotelguides Country Inn listing. So the
widely-repeated "heated indoor pool at 731 Citadel Rd" is **stale legacy data**, and both
current-brand sources say outdoor. A rebrand does not fill in a pool, so this is unresolved
rather than disproved — but it cannot be relied on.

Every brand site (ihg.com, choicehotels.com, marriott.com, hilton.com) returned 403 or timed
out, so **the authoritative source was never reachable.**

**Do not promise the boys a pool in Orangeburg. Call first:** Holiday Inn Express & Suites,
118 Sleep Inn Rd — "is the pool indoors, and open on November 28?" If yes, Orangeburg is a
solid stop on its own.

### Rooms that sleep four, and the properties to avoid

**Best of the Orangeburg cluster:**
- **Holiday Inn Express & Suites** (118 Sleep Inn Rd, 78 rooms) — 2-queen rooms, king+sofa, and
  1-bedroom suites with 2 queens or 2 doubles. **Free hot buffet breakfast.** From $109
  (Booking shows $132). TripAdvisor **3.7/5, 285 reviews, #5 of 23**; praise for breakfast and
  staff, recurring complaints about carpet and room maintenance.
- **Tru by Hilton** (739 Citadel Rd) — **best-rated in town: TripAdvisor 4.3/5, #2 of 23**,
  267 reviews. Hot breakfast buffet, lobby snacks, and a **pool table in the lobby** — a real
  draw for a 6- and 9-year-old on a cold evening. **No pool at all.** From ~$110. One severe
  review describes a prepaid room resold to a walk-in, leaving guests stranded overnight.
- **Hampton Inn & Suites** (749 Citadel Rd, 90 rooms) — **Booking 9.0/10 (198 reviews)**,
  the best-reviewed full-service option. 2-queen rooms (323 sq ft), studio suites with
  king+sofa. Free buffet breakfast, weekends 07:00–10:00. From ~$130. Pool is outdoor.
- **Spark by Hilton** (731 Citadel Rd, 81 rooms) — suites and rooms with 2 queens, fridge and
  microwave throughout, **continental** breakfast 06:00–09:30. Booking 7.9/10, from ~$98.

**Rule out:**
- **Comfort Inn & Suites, 746 Citadel Rd — the worst finding of the search. TripAdvisor 1.0/5,
  #19 of 23.** Both reviews "Terrible"; one describes a pet room as "filthy, dirty, reeks of
  dog urine so strong it is unbearable," the other a double-charge refunded only 50%. Listings
  also disagree on room count (69 vs 91), consistent with drift across an ownership change.
- **SpringHill Suites, 675 Citadel Rd** — Booking 8.7/10 but **TripAdvisor 3.5/5, #8 of 23**,
  with repeated housekeeping complaints: "unmade beds, wrinkled linens, torn wallpaper, visible
  mold," "moldy ice machines."
- Days Inn (3691 St Matthews Rd) — "very low, not recommended"; a review reports **bed bugs**.
- Super 8 (both locations), Quality Inn & Suites (3671 St Matthews Rd), Days Inn South — all
  rated "low" or "very low, not recommended" with sanitation complaints.

**Rate baseline:** Orangeburg County is not separately listed in the FY2026 federal per diem
tables, so it falls to the **Standard CONUS rate of $110**. Observed asking prices of $98–139
straddle that, i.e. **no visible Thanksgiving-weekend spike.**

### The actual trade — and the cost is larger than it looks

I measured it: **Edisto Memorial Gardens → Santee is 38 minutes / 26.5 miles**, not the ~20
minutes the research implied.

| | Sleep in Orangeburg | Sleep in Santee |
|---|---|---|
| Indoor pool | **Unconfirmed** (call first) | **Confirmed** (Tru, ~$107, free breakfast) |
| After the lights | 8–10 min to the hotel | **38 min night drive with tired kids** |
| Sunday via I-95 | 4:04 | **3:44** |
| Sunday avoiding I-95 | **4:19 (+15 min)** | 4:46 (+61 min) |

So Santee buys a confirmed pool and a 20-minute-shorter Sunday, but costs a 38-minute drive at
about 20:30 and **gives up the cheap I-95 dodge** — from Santee, avoiding I-95 costs an hour.

## The Children's Garden Christmas Walk — confirmed, with two caveats

Source throughout: <https://www.orangeburg.sc.us/edisto-memorial-gardens/childrens-garden-christmas-walk>

**Confirmed:**
- **Hours: seven days a week, 17:00–22:00.**
- **"There's no admission fee."** No donation request appears anywhere on the page.
- **"Over 60 still and animated displays"** on the drive-through along Riverside Drive, plus
  **"an additional 40 displays"** on a separate walk-on **Kids' Walk**. Both figures confirmed.
- **"Many of these displays are designed to be interactive."**
- Draws **300,000 guests a season** — expect traffic on a Saturday night.
- Gardens address **218 Riverside Dr SW** (the chamber gives 200 Riverside Dr SW).

**Caveat 1 — the dates are derived, not published.** The city states the season runs "from the
Monday preceding Thanksgiving through the dawn of New Year's Day." For 2026 that computes to
**Nov 23 – Jan 1**, which is what I reported — but **no year-specific 2026 calendar entry was
found.** The standing rule is the only basis.

**Caveat 2 — Santa on Nov 28 is NOT confirmed.** The city says Santa "can be found gracing the
gazebo on the Kids' Walk **on select days** leading up to Christmas between 18:00 and 20:00."
"Select days" is unspecified, and Nov 28 is four weeks before Christmas. **Do not promise it.**

**Could not determine** — not published anywhere reachable: whether there is a train, food
vendors or restrooms; the exact parking lot and walk-on entrance; how long it takes; stroller
and accessibility notes. The city's Parks & Rec platform (orangeburg.recdesk.com) returned no
readable content. **Call Parks & Recreation, (803) 533-6020.**

## The daylight window (15:19–17:16): the best feature is that it is the same place

**Edisto Memorial Gardens is open dawn to dusk, free, and the Christmas Walk starts at 17:00 in
the same park.** They can walk the gardens from 15:30, be at the Kids' Walk as it switches on,
and never move the car. That is a genuinely better shape than any other stop on the route.

- **Horne Wetlands Park — the 2,600-ft boardwalk.** Elevated walkway over tupelo-cypress swamp,
  with a boat dock and a gazebo for watching wildlife. Free, dawn to dusk, flat — so fine for
  short legs, and genuinely exciting at 6 and 9. **Do this in the light, before 17:00.**
  *Whether it is lit after dark is not published.*
- **Rose garden** — 187 labelled varieties, 5,000 plants, and the city's own description says
  blooms "stretch all the way to the Thanksgiving season," so some may still be out. Low appeal
  for boys, but it is on the way through.
- **Worth knowing before you walk into it:** the gardens also contain the **Angel of Hope
  Garden**, a memorial to children who have died. Not something to encounter unprepared with
  two small boys.

**Indoor options are all unavailable or unknown:**
- **I.P. Stanback Museum & Planetarium**, SC State University (40-ft dome, 82 seats) would be
  the ideal indoor option for these ages — but **SCSU publishes no hours, days, admission or
  show times**, and **Nov 28 is the Saturday of the university's Thanksgiving break**. Assume
  closed unless you call: 704-649-6604.
- **Orangeburg County Fine Arts Center** — **no working website**; its domains do not resolve
  and one is parked for sale. Facebook-only presence, unfetchable.
- **Cecil Williams South Carolina Civil Rights Museum** — site has a **broken TLS certificate**
  and will not load. Likely heavy going for a 6-year-old regardless.
- **Claflin University / Arthur Rose Museum** — every URL pattern 404'd.
- **Orangeburg County Historical Society** (1421 Middleton St, Sat 10:00–16:00, closed
  12:00–13:00) is a **genealogical archive** — 4,000 family names, family-history research.
  Skip it.
- **No playground could be confirmed** anywhere in the city's parks — the Parks & Rec site is
  unreadable and no source names one at Edisto Memorial Gardens.
- Santee Recreation & Water Park is **outdoor** — closed in November.

## Dinner

Standing hours; none confirmed for Nov 28 specifically except where noted.

1. **House of Pizza / The Barrel Room**, 1375 Sims St, (803) 534-7500 — **Sat 06:30–21:30**,
   bar to last call. American/Italian/Greek, explicitly family-friendly, and an **in-house
   ice cream parlour**. **"Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day" only — so open Nov 28**, the
   one place with a published holiday policy. **Safest pick, and the right shape: eat after the
   lights, ice cream included.**
2. **Tokyo Steak & Seafood**, 1338 Grove Park Dr, (803) 533-0792 — **Sat 11:00–22:30**, the
   latest close in town. **Hibachi cooked at the table**, plus a kids' menu, ~$30/person.
   3.8/5 (192 reviews). **Highest entertainment value for these ages.**
3. **Chestnut Grill**, 1455 Chestnut St — **Sat 16:00–21:00 only** (closed Sun and Mon).
   4.1/5, 352 reviews, family-owned since 1987.
4. **Pauline's Dining Room**, 1145 Orangeburg Mall Circle — **the highest-rated local
   restaurant found, 4.4/5 (273 reviews)**, Southern all-you-can-eat buffet. But **Sat
   12:00–19:30**, so it closes mid-Christmas-Walk. Good only if you flip the order and eat early.

**Small-town caveat confirmed:** only two local sit-down restaurants are open past 20:00 on a
Saturday, and two are closed Sunday entirely. Chains present: Chick-fil-A, Bojangles, Waffle
House, Papa Johns, Hardee's, Arby's, Subway.

## Nothing else on Nov 28 — confirmed across three calendars

- **Orangeburg Christmas Parade is Sunday Dec 6, 16:00–19:00**, Russell Street, theme
  "Christmas in Toyland" — eight days late.
- The **County Chamber calendar shows no events for Nov 27, 28 or 29.**
- The **City's own site lists nothing after Nov 19.** No tree lighting, market or festival.
- **No Orangeburg tree lighting was found for 2026 at all.**

So the Christmas Walk is the whole evening — and at 17:00–22:00, free, in the same park they
spent the afternoon in, that is the right scale for the slot.
