## Florence SC — sleep only, confirmed empty

- Downtown holiday season **has not started**: Holiday Music & Light Show runs **Dec 4–31**;
  tree lighting **Dec 4**; Christmas parade **Dec 5**. Arrive exactly one week early.
  <https://www.florencedowntown.com/holiday-offerings-downtown-florence>
- Florence County Museum: free, **closed Saturdays** per its own Visit page.
  <https://www.flocomuseum.org/visit/>
- Drs. Bruce & Lee Foundation Library (good children's area): **Sat 09:00–13:00**. Shut.
  <https://www.florencelibrary.org/locations-hours>
- Lodging is the cheapest on the corridor: Holiday Inn Express I-95/I-20 has a year-round
  **indoor saltwater pool**, listed from ~$91; Comfort Suites indoor pool ~$82.
  Citywide listings from ~$40–53. (Listed starting rates, not Nov 28 2026 quotes.)
  <https://www.ihg.com/holidayinnexpress/hotels/us/en/florence/flosc/hoteldetail>

**Verdict: the honest "just sleep" option. Zero content for the kids beyond a hotel pool.**

## Fayetteville NC — consolation program

- **Sweet Valley Ranch Festival of Lights, Nov 28 OPEN, 18:30–22:00.** 1M+ lights
  drive-through plus a walk-around with glowing dinosaurs, inflatables, fires, a "Santavator"
  ride. **$48 online for 4 ($12 each) / $68 at the gate.** Genuinely strong for ages 6 and 9.
  <https://www.sweetvalleyranchnc.com/festival-of-lights>
- Fayetteville Holiday Lights at Segra Stadium: **Nov 28 open**, ~$11–13/person
  (≈$45–53 for four, age breakdown not published). **Nightly gate hours not published
  anywhere — must call.** Opening-night fireworks are Nov 27, so they are missed.
  <https://www.fayettevilleholidaylights.com/tickets-schedule>
- **A Dickens Holiday is Friday Nov 27, not Nov 28.** Missed by one day.
  <https://www.adickensholiday.com/>
- Cape Fear Botanical "Holiday Lights in the Garden": **Dec 17–21**. Ruled out.
  <https://www.visitnc.com/events/holiday-lights-garden-2>
- Daytime options all shut before an 17:47 arrival: Fascinate-U Children's Museum
  (Sat 10–17, ~$24 for four), Airborne & Special Operations Museum (free, Tue–Sat 10–16,
  and Sunday opens at noon so it can't be caught on the way out either).
- Lodging: Embassy Suites Fort Bragg — indoor pool, **two-room suites**, ~$119–134.

**Verdict: Sweet Valley Ranch is a real draw, but every signature event is Nov 27 and
every daytime attraction is closed on arrival.**

## Southern Pines / Pinehurst NC — best content, worst position

- **Sandhills Garden of Lights — inaugural year. Nov 28 OPEN, 17:00–22:00.** 1M+ lights,
  interactive light orbs, ~0.4-mile flat walk-through, **Santa present Nov 27–29**.
  Free parking with shuttles. <https://www.sandhills.edu/horticultural-gardens/garden-of-lights.html>
  - **Price not published** — explicitly demand-based timed entry. Structure: under 2 free,
    3–11 child, 12+ adult; 17:00 and 21:30 slots cheapest. **Call 910-695-3884.**
  - **Book now:** inaugural year + Thanksgiving Saturday + timed entry = sell-out risk.
- Southern Pines downtown tree lighting **the same evening**: Santa by fire truck 16:30,
  **tree lit 18:15** at the Sunrise Theater green. Free.
  - **UNCERTAIN:** sourced to three local blogs/papers; **absent from southernpines.net's
    own calendar**. PineStraw confuses it with the Pinehurst lighting (Dec 4). Confirm.
- Weymouth Woods Sandhills Nature Preserve: free, but **November close is 18:00** (visitor
  centre 17:00). Useless on arrival — but a good **Sunday-morning** 45-min stop before the
  1:09 hop home. <https://www.ncparks.gov/state-parks/weymouth-woods-sandhills-nature-preserve>
- Walkable Broad St for dinner; The Ice Cream Parlor (soda fountain, burgers/hot dogs) is
  the obvious kid pick. Shops shut by arrival.
- Lodging: Hampton Inn & Suites Southern Pines–Pinehurst (indoor pool, ~$120–235);
  SpringHill Suites (family rooms, from ~$149). Budget options nearby appear to have
  **outdoor** pools — verify.

**Verdict: the richest Nov 28 program found anywhere on the route, and the shortest Sunday
(1:09). But a 7:19 Saturday drive arriving 18:09 is a long day for a 6- and 9-year-old and
abandons the halfway premise entirely. Off-spec, but worth putting to the owner.**

## Myrtle Beach SC — rejected on geometry

- **Brookgreen Gardens "Nights of a Thousand Candles" is SOLD OUT for Nov 28** (fully
  booked: Nov 27–28, Dec 4–5, 11–12, 18–19). The single best reason to detour is gone.
  <https://whosonthemove.com/act-fast-brookgreen-gardens-adds-new-dates-for-nights-of-a-thousand-candles-as-peak-nights-sell-out/>
- Still open Nov 28 and genuinely good: **Great Christmas Light Show**, N Myrtle Beach,
  17:30–21:30, **$25 per car**, and Nov 28 is a Santa's Village night (petting zoo, Santa).
  <https://www.nmb.us/680/Christmas-in-North-Myrtle-Beach>
  Free **Winter Wonderland at the Beach** boardwalk lights nightly 17:00–21:00; free
  **Intracoastal Christmas Regatta** lit-boat parade **Nov 28 ~17:00**; Ripley's Aquarium
  reportedly open to 22:00 Sat ($140 for four — third-party sourced, unconfirmed).
- Lodging is the cheapest anywhere: indoor-waterpark resorts (Dunes Village, Crown Reef)
  and oceanfront indoor pools listed ~$65–112; citywide from ~$46.

**Verdict: objectively the richest after-dark menu and cheapest beds, but +2:09 detour and
a 3:40 Sunday. Only rational as a two-night destination, not a waypoint. Rejected.**
