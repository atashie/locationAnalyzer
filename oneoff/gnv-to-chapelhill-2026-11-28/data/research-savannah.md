# Research: Savannah GA (+ Pooler, Tybee)

Position: **Sat 3:17 / arrive 13:50 / Sun 5:16 (335 mi) / detour +0:15 / frac 0.38.**
Cheap to reach, lots of daylight on arrival, and a confirmed free evening programme —
but it is **too early in the trip**, and the 5:16 Sunday runs the length of I-95.

## Confirmed open Sat Nov 28 2026

- **Holiday Kickoff at Eastern Wharf + 25th Annual Savannah Lighted Boat Parade — FREE.**
  Kickoff 17:00–21:00, boats 19:00–21:00, The Park at Eastern Wharf (201 Port St).
  Tree lighting, Santa, food trucks, **30+ lit boats looping twice to the Talmadge Bridge**.
  This is the single best free fit found anywhere on the route: entirely after dark, flexible
  length, and lit boats land well with a 6- and 9-year-old.
  <https://savannahswaterfront.com/event/holiday-kickoff-at-eastern-wharf/>
- **December Nights & Holiday Lights**, Coastal Georgia Botanical Gardens — **Nov 28 is
  opening night**. Arrival windows 17:30–20:00, **$15/person advance ($20 door), under 3
  free → $60 for four**. Timed entry, buy online. ~1-hour walk-through, 12 mi west of
  downtown. <https://coastalbg.uga.edu/2026/10/december-nights-and-holiday-lights-2026/>
- **Savannah Christmas Market**, Plant Riverside — daily 11:00–21:00 through Jan 3, **free**.
  <https://www.plantriverside.com/savannah-christmas-market/>
- **Nutcracker**, SC Ballet, 17:30 Johnny Mercer Theatre — from $72/ticket (**~$288 for
  four**). Priced out, and it collides with the free boat parade.
- Tybee Island tree lighting, Strand parking lot — time not published on reachable sources.

## Daytime window (13:50–17:16) is thinner than expected

Only two attractions actually survive the arrival time:

- **Mighty Eighth Air Force Museum** (Pooler, ~10 mi, right off I-95) — indoor, **closes
  17:00**, **$44** for four. The best daylight-window option, and conveniently placed.
- **Fort Pulaski NM** (~13 mi) — fort **09:00–16:30**, Cockspur Island gate to 17:00,
  **$20/vehicle**. Open Nov 28 on the standing rule (closed only Thanksgiving/Christmas/
  New Year's) but **no 2026-dated notice exists and a 2023 release announced an early
  Thanksgiving close** — verify. Authoritative hours live in the Superintendent's
  Compendium, which renders when the JS hours pages do not:
  <https://www.nps.gov/fopu/learn/management/compendium.htm>

Ruled out on hours:
- **Georgia State Railroad Museum** — closes 16:00.
- **Oatland Island Wildlife Center** — **vehicle cutoff 15:30**, and it is **closed Nov 26–27**.
  Admission $24 for four ($7 adult / $5 child 4–17).
- **Bonaventure Cemetery** — gates close 17:00, and its own posted rules require "no running,
  playing or loud noises." Not a children's activity on the operator's terms.
- **Old Town Trolley** — last full narrated tour departs 16:30 and is **drop-off only, no
  hop-on/hop-off**. The holiday "Festive Nights" tour is $113.80 for four and collides with
  the free boat parade.

**Pirates' House** has a real "For Little Pirates Under 10" menu (both boys qualify), seven
entrées $6.50–$10.50 → family food ≈ $63–$127 before tax and tip.
<https://thepirateshouse.com/kids-menu/>

## Verdict

**Best free evening programme on the entire route, and nearly free to reach — but the
geometry is wrong.** At frac 0.38 it front-loads the easy day and leaves 335 miles of I-95
for the Sunday of Thanksgiving weekend. Choose Savannah only if maximising the exploration
matters more than the Sunday drive.

## Additions from the full report

in Pooler**. **No Nov 28 2026 live rate was retrievable for any property** — Marriott,
Hilton, Hyatt, IHG, Choice and Booking.com all returned 403 or JS-only content. Figures
below are generic "from" rates, not holiday quotes.

- **Drury Plaza Savannah Pooler** (500 E US-80) — *best overall fit.* Indoor pool +
  whirlpool 09:00–22:00, free hot breakfast, **free 5:30 Kickback hot food and drinks
  nightly**, free parking, two-queen rooms. **Dinner, breakfast and parking all cost
  nothing** — three line items removed.
  <https://www.druryhotels.com/locations/savannah-ga/drury-plaza-hotel-savannah-pooler>
- **Holiday Inn Express & Suites Hardeeville SC** (I-95 **Exit 8**) — *best for the Sunday

- Cheap Pooler fallbacks, all indoor pool + free hot breakfast: Baymont $68, Sleep Inn $72,

### Parking — there is a trap
The City's free-meter holiday list covers **Nov 26 and 27 but NOT Saturday Nov 28**. Meters
run Mon–Sat 08:00–20:00 north of Liberty at up to $2.50/hr; Sundays free.
**Use a garage: $5 flat all-day weekends** at Bryan Street, Liberty, Robinson and State
Street — but **special events bill at $25/day**, and Eastern Wharf is $10, so Holiday Kickoff
night may price up. "Flat all-day" appears to bill per calendar day, so an overnight likely
bills twice. <https://www.savannahga.gov/2516/Parking-Garages>
**Tybee parking is free Thanksgiving Day through New Year's Day**, island-wide (normal rate
$4/hr or $25/day). <https://www.cityoftybee.org/386/Visitors>

### The Sunday traffic warning, quantified
AAA has published no 2026 forecast yet. Its 2025 edition projected **81.8 million travelers,
~73 million by car**, with INRIX advising that Sunday returns face **heavy traffic most of
the day — worst 11:00–20:00, best before 11:00**.
<https://newsroom.aaa.com/2025/11/aaa-thanksgiving-travel-forecast-2025/>
Savannah sits on the worst stretch of the Florida-to-Northeast return flow. **If staying
here, be on I-95 north before 10:00.** Hardeeville (Exit 8) rebalances the split slightly.

### Two more good free options
- **Savannah Belles Ferry** — a real boat ride across the river, every 10 min, **07:00–22:00**,
  free. Best cost-to-delight ratio downtown for a 6-year-old.
- **Plant Riverside Generator Hall** — free museum tours daily, with 4–5-million-year-old
  geodes, mineral collections and a **full-scale dinosaur replica**. (This is the real
  exhibit; "Electric Moon" is a rooftop bar.)

### Caution on the evening shape
The events do not peak until 19:00 (boats). After a four-hour drive that is a long night for
a 6-year-old — the **17:00 tree-lighting/Santa/food-truck slice is the realistic portion**.
