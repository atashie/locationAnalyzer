### Saluda Shoals — Holiday Lights on the River (best value anywhere on the route)
6071 St Andrews Rd. Nightly Nov 25–Dec 31, 18:00–22:00. **Nov 28 is a "Yellow" night at
$30 per car** — and Green/Yellow nights are explicitly flagged as lighter traffic and
shorter waits. Three miles of animated drive-through displays.
**Everything included, no extra charges:** Winter Wonder dual tube slides, hayride, train
ride, laser light show on the Wetland Trail, Letter-to-Santa craft, gift shop, concessions.
**Santa meet-and-greets confirmed Nov 27–29.** Primarily experienced from the warm car with
walk-up activities you opt into — no forced march for tired kids. Tickets are
**night-specific, no refunds or transfers.** <https://www.icrc.net/event/holiday-lights-river-1>

### Riverbanks Wild Lights (the marquee option)
Formerly "Lights Before Christmas," renamed **Wild Lights**: 60+ handcrafted wildlife
lanterns, nightly snowfall, Santa through Dec 23, carousel, train, moonlight swings,
illuminated stepping tiles. **Nov 28 confirmed 17:00–21:00 on the zoo's own calendar.**
<https://www.riverbanks.org/events/wild-lights>
- Walkable, 1–2 hours at a kid's pace, and **some animals are genuinely visible at night** —
  visitors report koalas, zebras, flamingos, squirrel monkeys, sea lions.
- Admission is **date-tiered $20.99–25.99 adult / $15.99–20.99 child**, under 2 free. Nov 28
  is peak Thanksgiving Saturday, so expect the top of the band → **~$74 low / ~$94 high**.
  Parking $3 online / $6 gate. Optional Twinkle Rides Pass $8 (unlimited train + carousel).
- **The exact Nov 28 tier could not be confirmed** — the ticketing portal returns 403. The
  range is published; the figures are arithmetic on it, not a quote.
- **The daytime zoo closes at 16:00** in winter (Nov 2–Jan 31), so a 16:01 arrival cannot do
  the day zoo at all — only Wild Lights. Separate tickets.
- **Book now:** tickets went on sale Nov 2, capacity is limited, peak night.
- Brew Lights (21+) is Dec 4 only — not a conflict.

### Fireflies Holiday Lights
Prisma Health Park, 1640 Freed St. Nov 20–Jan 3, 18:00–21:00 nightly, **open Nov 28**.
**$10/person in November** (+$2 day-of), under 2 free. Includes the **Fireflies Express
train ride** (12 and under), s'mores pits, two light tunnels, a 40-ft tree, and **Santa
nightly 18:30–20:30**. 2M+ lights.

### Glide & Gather ice rink
Richland County Rec seasonal outdoor rink, **North Springs Park, 1320 Clemson Road**
(Northeast Columbia, not downtown). Nov 20–Jan 3, Sat 11:00–21:00. **$15 adult / $10 child
12 and under**, including skate rental and an hour on the ice; skates from child 7T up.
A real novelty for Florida boys.
<https://www.richlandcountyrecreation.com/360/Glide-Gather-Ice-Skating-Rink>

### Free bonus
**Lisznyai Christmas House**, 725 Formby Dr (Spring Valley) — synchronized display,
**17:30–22:30 nightly from Thanksgiving to Dec 31**, tune 88.7 FM, free.

## Lodging

**No home football game.** USC plays **at Clemson** Nov 28 — verified four ways. Its last
home game was Nov 21 vs Georgia. **Book Columbia, not the Clemson/Greenville corridor**,
which will be jammed. The only venue event is **women's basketball Nov 28 at 14:30** vs
Tennessee State — a low-draw opponent at an early tip, so **moderate pressure on
Vista/downtown rooms only**, not market-wide.

**Rate level:** **GSA federal per diem for Columbia is $115, flat all twelve months** — the
rate-setter treats the city as non-seasonal. Charleston is $218–288. Market ADR has run
~$105–120 through 2024–Q1 2026 (Colliers/CoStar). Expect ~12% tax.
*Caveat:* "cheaper than coastal" is true annually but **not against Myrtle Beach ($110) or
Hilton Head ($118) that specific week** — both sit at seasonal floors. Only Charleston stays
clearly above Columbia.

**Neighbourhoods:** **Harbison/Irmo (I-26 Exit 102A)** is best for tired kids — deepest hotel
cluster, Dave & Buster's 0.1 mi, Frankie's Fun Park, chain dining, one parking lot; Irmo PD
logged 0 murders in 2024. **Cayce/West Columbia** is statistically safest (Lexington County's
violent-crime rate is roughly half Richland's) but thin on lodging. **NE Columbia/I-77 Exit
17** is best for Sunday's departure. The Vista has the best dinner and most basketball
exposure.
*Avoid:* **Decker Blvd** — the Sheriff shut down a nightclub there after a four-victim
shooting in Sept 2026. No family hotels there anyway.
*One common warning that does not survive checking:* "avoid Two Notch Road" — in Columbia
PD's own arrest data it ranks *below* Harbison and Columbiana. The documented risk is
**pedestrian fatalities** (three in 2025), not violent crime. So: don't plan to walk across
Two Notch; driving it is unremarkable.

- **Orangeburg is the one real contender.** The **Children's Garden Christmas Walk** at
  Edisto Memorial Gardens runs **Nov 23 – Jan 1, seven nights a week, 17:00–22:00, no
  admission fee** — both a drive-through with 60+ displays *and* a walk-on "Kids' Walk" with
  40 more. Free, kid-specific, confirmed. Also a free 2,600-ft wetland boardwalk, dawn–dusk.
  Costs: the **longest Sunday of the four (~4:04)** because you backtrack east, and **no
  Orangeburg lodging rate could be confirmed at all.**
  <https://www.orangeburg.sc.us/edisto-memorial-gardens/childrens-garden-christmas-walk>
- **Santee** sits exactly at the I-95/I-26 junction (zero detour) with the shortest Saturday.
  Santee State Park is $3/adult, $1/child, open 06:00–dark, cabins over Lake Marion. But the
  **NWR visitor center is closed Saturdays** and after 17:15 there is nothing for kids.
  Eight budget I-95 properties — *probably* cheapest, but **no price evidence**.
- **Camden** buys the best Sunday (3:13) but every attraction closes by 16:30: Historic
  Camden 10–16:30 (**Nov 28 status unconfirmed — call 803-432-9841**), Steeplechase Museum
  Wed–Sat 10–16, Archives 10–16. **The Colonial Cup was Nov 22.** Free Goodale State Park
  (1.5-mi sandy trail) is the one thing that fits. Lodging is thin (~5–8 properties), itself
  a holiday-weekend risk, and the Sunday leg is 127 mi of **US-1 surface highway**, which
  feels slower with kids than the clock suggests.
- **Florence strictly dominates Camden** on driving: near-identical Saturday, **30 min
  shorter Sunday**, never leaving I-95.
