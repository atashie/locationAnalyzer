## Hilton Head Island — the only Lowcountry stop with content on Nov 28

- **Harbour Town Lights: Nov 27 2026 – Jan 1 2027.** Illuminated seasonal figures + a
  large tree, tagged kid-friendly, free to stroll. **Nov 28 is night two.**
  <https://www.hiltonheadisland.org/events/2026-harbour-town-lights>
- **Shelter Cove Holiday Lights + 28-ft tree: Nov 23 – Jan 7**, lit **19:00–20:00 nightly**.
  <https://www.welcometosheltercove.com/calendar-of-events>
- **Harbour Town daytime:** Gregg Russell playground is a **treehouse built around a live
  oak** (free); lighthouse is a 114-step climb with a small museum, open to sundown (~18:00).
  **$10 Sea Pines vehicle gate + $32 lighthouse = $42**, parking free.
- Coastal Discovery Museum: **free**, 70 acres, Mon–Sat to 16:30 — but its site says
  "call for holiday hours," **unconfirmed for Nov 28** (843-689-6767).
- **Skip Pinckney Island NWR**: Ibis Pond is a 2-mile round trip with no restrooms, water
  or shelter — wrong call for a 6-year-old near dusk.
- Santa at Shelter Cove Nov 28 13:30–15:30 is **contested** — the venue calendar lists
  Nov 27 *and* 28, the CVB lists only Nov 27. Call.
- **Lodging is the problem.** Resort market with real fees: Sonesta $40+$3/day; Omni ~$45/day
  resort fee **plus $32–50 parking**, from $439. Indoor-pool floor ~$127–138. The only
  property with a credible **indoor heated** pool and two queens is the **Hilton Beachfront
  Resort** (1 Hotel Circle) — and one third-party source lists outdoor only, so **confirm by
  phone**. Marriott's Grande Ocean has 2-BR villas with kitchen/laundry; owner-rented
  VRBO/Airbnb listings are usually well under the developer rate.

**Verdict: the only confirmed kid-facing after-dark attraction in the Lowcountry on this
exact date, and it is opening weekend. But it costs the most and leaves a 5:20 Sunday.**

## Beaufort — prettiest, best value, and it misses by one week

- **Beaufort's holiday weekend is Dec 4–6 2026**: Night on the Town Fri Dec 4 (tree lighting
  20:30), Light Up the Night Boat Parade Sat Dec 5, Christmas Parade Sun Dec 6. **Nothing on
  Nov 28.** Mistletoe Market ran Nov 19–22. Nov 28 falls in the dead gap.
  <https://mainstreetbeaufort.org/events/>
- Free and genuinely good for ages 6 and 9, all **closing at dusk (~17:45)**:
  - **Port Royal Cypress Wetlands** — 0.6-mi boardwalk loop, **alligators, turtles, herons**,
    a "Kids in Parks" TRACK Trail with free activity brochures, restrooms on site. Best
    single kid hour in Beaufort. <https://www.sctrails.net/trails/trail/cypress-wetlands>
  - **Henry C. Chambers Waterfront Park** — large modern playground, restrooms adjacent,
    bench swings over the river. Free parking.
  - **The Sands boardwalk**, Port Royal — observation tower, **shark-tooth hunting**.
- **Hunting Island is only 16 mi / ~25–30 min east of downtown** (not 45–60 as I assumed),
  so ~65–75 min off I-95 total. Lighthouse **reopened May 2026** after a 4-year restoration;
  167 steps, 44-inch minimum (both boys clear). **$24 park + $20 lighthouse = $44.**
  - **Timing trap: last lighthouse climb 16:30, park gate closes 18:00 in November.** You
    must leave I-95 by ~15:15. Later than that and you pay $24 to walk a cold beach.
- Parris Island Museum: free, Mon–Sat to 16:30, strong draw for a 9-year-old — but needs
  **REAL ID + vehicle registration + proof of insurance** at the gate.
- **Unconfirmed but high-value if real:** downtown holiday projections (Nov 24–Dec 25,
  18:00–22:00) and the free **Cadd Family walk-through light display** (66 Springfield Rd,
  Lady's Island, "usually kicking off right at Thanksgiving"). Both appear only on a page
  whose own header says **2025**. Call the city: **843-379-7519**.
- **Lodging is the best value of the four** — indoor pools ~$76–99, no resort fee, roughly a
  third of Hilton Head. Comfort Suites near MCAS (131 Big John Rd) is best-reviewed
  (4.0/5, 636 reviews): indoor pool + hot tub, 420 sq ft all-suite, 2 queens + twin sofa,
  free hot breakfast. Downtown's charming inns have **no** indoor pool.

**Verdict: conditional. Beautiful, cheap, great free daytime attractions — but the evening
is empty and the holiday weekend is a week late. Only worth it if you arrive by ~16:00.**

## Walterboro — zero detour, zero content

- **Nothing on Nov 28.** Walterboro's tree lighting is **Dec 3, 17:30** at City Hall.
- **All three interesting indoor things close at 14:00 Saturday** — before arrival:
  Walterboro Wildlife Center (the actual kid attraction, Sat 10–14), Colleton Museum
  (Sat 10–14), and Hiott's Pharmacy old-time soda fountain (Sat 9–13).
- What remains: **Walterboro Wildlife Sanctuary / Great Swamp Sanctuary** — free, 800+ acres
  of blackwater creek, **boardwalks and bridges**, cypress and Spanish moss, beaver and duck
  ponds. Genuinely lovely at golden hour. Dawn to dusk.
  <https://www.walterborosc.org/walterboro-wildlife-sanctuary-0>
- **No indoor pool anywhere in town.** Comfort Inn & Suites I-95 (97 Downs Ln) is the value
  pick — 2 queens, free breakfast buffet, observed **$88–90** in mid-November — but its pool
  is **outdoor, May–September**. (Google's "indoor pool" claim for it is **false**; two
  independent sources say seasonal outdoor.) Hampton Inn is nicest at $182–216, outdoor
  unheated pool. Holiday Inn Express has **no pool**.
- Nearest indoor pool: **Best Western Point South**, Yemassee, **I-95 Exit 33**, year-round
  indoor pool 08:30–19:00, from $74 — and that is the same exit you would use for Beaufort.

**Verdict: thin. The best pure logistics on the route — no detour, ~$90, free breakfast, and
one lovely free hour on a cypress boardwalk — but not a memory.**

## Summerville — skip

- **Nothing on Nov 28** (three calendars checked). Old Time Christmas Tree Celebration is
  **Tue Dec 1**; Christmas on the Square Dec 3–18; parade Dec 13. All after departure.
- **Colonial Dorchester State Historic Site** is the best-value kid attraction found in the
  Lowcountry: ruins of a town abandoned in the 1770s, a **tabby fort made of oyster-shell
  concrete**, an Anglican bell tower, a tidal wharf on the Ashley visible at low tide, flat
  easy paths — **$8 for the family**. But it **closes at 17:00** in November.
  <https://southcarolinaparks.com/colonial-dorchester>
- The geometry kills it: ~29 miles off I-95 **as a dead-end you drive twice** (~90 min
  round trip) for one site you can only catch if you arrive by 16:00.
- Home2 Suites (221 Holiday Dr) has the region's one confirmed **indoor heated saline pool**,
  09:00–23:00, all-suite with sofa bed and kitchen, free hot breakfast, ~$147–218.
- **Two false indoor-pool claims corrected:** Spark by Hilton Summerville and Comfort Suites
  Summerville are both **outdoor**. The whole Nexton cluster is outdoor-only.
- "Summerville Skates" ice rink is **likely defunct** — Yelp lists it closed and Parks & Rec
  doesn't mention it. 843-821-7260 ext 700.

**Verdict: skip. Good attraction, wrong geometry, holiday programming three days too late.**

## Caveats carried forward

- **No nightly rate in this research is a confirmed Nov 28 2026 quote.** Hilton, Marriott,
  IHG, Choice and Expedia all returned 403; aggregators silently priced near-term dates.
- **Airbnb/VRBO was never covered** — the session web-search budget ran out first.
- Indoor-pool inventory in these markets is tiny (three hotels in Beaufort, essentially one
  on Hilton Head, one in Summerville, **none in Walterboro**) and those are exactly the
  properties families converge on over a holiday weekend. Book early.
- "Late November is cheap on Hilton Head" is an **untested prior** — no source supports it.
