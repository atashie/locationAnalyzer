# Recommendation — overnight stop, Sat 28 Nov 2026

Companion to `analysis.md` (routing and daylight) and the five files in `data/`.

## The one fact that reorganised everything

**Almost every town on this route starts its holiday season the first week of December.**

| Town | Tree lighting / parade | Relative to Nov 28 |
|---|---|---|
| Summerville SC | Dec 1 | 3 days late |
| Walterboro SC | Dec 3 | 5 days late |
| Beaufort SC | Dec 4–6 | 6–8 days late |
| Florence SC | Dec 4 (lights), Dec 5 (parade) | 6–7 days late |
| Charleston SC | Dec 5–6 | 7–8 days late |
| Columbia SC | Dec 5 (parade) | 7 days late |
| Fayetteville NC | **Nov 27** (Dickens Holiday) | **1 day early** |
| Savannah GA | Nov 27 (downtown tree) | 1 day early |

Nov 28 2026 falls in a gap: a week before the parades, a day after the kickoffs. So the
question is not "which town is nicest" — it is **which places have something actually running
that specific night.** That shrinks a 38-stop list to about six, and most of what survives
is a Christmas light display that opened on Thanksgiving weekend.

The second fact: **you arrive in the dark.** Sunset is 17:10–17:20 across the whole band, and
anything past Walterboro gives you under two hours of daylight. Every daytime attraction on
this route closes between 16:00 and 17:00. **Fighting that is a losing game — the right move
is to pick a stop where darkness is the attraction.**

## Recommended: COLUMBIA, SC

**Sat 5:31 drive → arrive 16:01 · Sun 3:37 / 231 mi · detour +0:49 · 60% of the trip**

Four reasons, in order of how much they should matter to you:

**1. It is the only stop that avoids I-95 on Sunday 29 November.** Columbia departs north on
I-77 → I-85 → I-40, through Charlotte and Greensboro. Every other candidate funnels onto
I-95 through the Fayetteville corridor — the worst northbound stretch in the country on the
heaviest return-travel day of the year. AAA's 2025 figures were 81.8 million travelers,
~73 million by car, with INRIX putting the worst window at **11:00–20:00**. Columbia also
gives the shortest Sunday of any in-band stop (3:37). Charlotte and Greensboro have their own
holiday backups, so budget 4 hours — but it is a different and lighter risk than I-95.

**2. Three separate light events are confirmed open that evening, at three price points.**
You do not need a backup plan; you need to pick one.

| Option | Hours | Cost, family of 4 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Saluda Shoals, Holiday Lights on the River** | **18:00–22:00** | **$30/car, all-in** | **My pick.** 3 miles of animated drive-through, and Nov 28 is officially a "Yellow" (lighter-traffic) night. Includes **dual tube slides, hayride, train, laser show on the Wetland Trail, Letter-to-Santa craft** at no extra charge, with **Santa confirmed Nov 27–29**. Experienced mostly from a warm car with opt-in stops — exactly right after five and a half hours of driving. |
| **Riverbanks Wild Lights** | **17:00–21:00** | ~$74–97 + $3 parking | The better *experience* if the boys still have energy: 60+ handcrafted wildlife lanterns, nightly snowfall, Santa, carousel, train, and **some animals genuinely visible at night** (koalas, flamingos, sea lions). Price is date-tiered and Nov 28 is peak; the exact tier could not be confirmed. |
| **Fireflies Holiday Lights**, Segra Park | **18:00–21:00**, last entry 20:30 | **$40** | 2M+ lights in the ballpark, **train ride for under-12s**, s'mores pits, light tunnels, 40-ft tree, **Santa nightly 18:30–20:30**. |

Plus two extras: **Glide & Gather outdoor ice rink** (North Springs Park, Sat 11:00–21:00,
**$50** including skates — a real novelty for Florida boys) and the free **Lisznyai Christmas
House** synchronized display (725 Formby Dr, 17:30–22:30 nightly, tune 88.7 FM).

**Do one, not two.** Wild Lights 17:00–18:45 then Saluda Shoals 19:30–21:00 is physically
possible and too much for a 6-year-old at the end of a travel day.

**3. There is no home football game.** USC plays **at Clemson** on Nov 28 — verified four
ways. The Clemson/Greenville corridor will be jammed; Columbia will not. The only venue
event is **women's basketball at 14:30** against a low-draw opponent, which puts moderate
pressure on Vista/downtown rooms only, not the wider market.

**4. Lodging is cheap, verified, and genuinely family-shaped.** GSA's federal per diem for
Columbia is **$115 flat across all twelve months** — the rate-setter treats the city as
non-seasonal. Market ADR has run ~$105–120.

- **Homewood Suites, 230 Greystone Blvd — best fit.** Heated **indoor** pool, free hot
  breakfast, all-suite with kitchens, **0.7 mi from Riverbanks Zoo**, ~$108–113.
- **Comfort Suites, 1540 Daulton Dr (I-77 Exit 17)** — heated indoor pool, free breakfast,
  all-suite, free parking, ~$123. **Best for Sunday's departure** — effectively on the ramp —
  and close to the ice rink.
- **Home2 Suites Harbison** (I-26 Exit 102A), ~$96–124 — nearest to Saluda Shoals, in the
  deepest hotel cluster, with Dave & Buster's and chain dining in one parking lot.

**Corrections worth knowing:** Hilton Columbia Center, Hampton Inn Harbison, Courtyard
Downtown and every Holiday Inn Express in the market have **outdoor** pools — useless in late
November. Hyatt Place has **no pool**. **Embassy Suites Columbia Greystone appears not to
exist** any more. Avoid Days Inn & Suites Garners Ferry (2.0/5 on 627 reviews, sanitation).
Given all-suite hotels at ~$100–160 with a bedroom, kitchen, breakfast, parking and indoor
pool, the Airbnb case here is weak.

**What Columbia does not have:** a tree lighting or parade that weekend (Vista Lights was
Nov 19, the Governor's Carolighting Nov 23, the parade Dec 5), no Nutcracker performance on
Nov 28 specifically, and Soda City Market ends at 13:00, three hours before you arrive.
Congaree's boardwalk is **fully reopened as of May 2026** and flat/railed/stroller-friendly
with no mosquitoes in November — but it is unlit and 30 minutes out. **Save it for a morning.**

## Runner-up, and the better choice if cost is the priority: ORANGEBURG, SC

**Sat 4:56 → arrive 15:19 · Sun 4:04 / 250 mi · detour +0:41 · 55% of the trip**

**Children's Garden Christmas Walk**, Edisto Memorial Gardens — **Nov 23 – Jan 1, seven
nights a week, 17:00–22:00, free.** It is both a drive-through with 60+ displays *and* a
walk-on "Kids' Walk" with 40 more. Free, kid-specific, date-confirmed.
<https://www.orangeburg.sc.us/edisto-memorial-gardens/childrens-garden-christmas-walk>
The park also has a free 2,600-ft wetland boardwalk, dawn to dusk — and you arrive at 15:19
with nearly two hours of light, **42 minutes more daylight than Columbia**.

It is also **8 minutes further inside your detour budget** than Columbia.

Two real costs: the **longest Sunday of any in-band stop (4:04, on I-95)**, because you
backtrack east to pick up the interstate; and **no Orangeburg lodging rate could be confirmed
at all**, which in a small town on Thanksgiving weekend is a genuine booking risk rather than
a formality.

**Take Orangeburg if a free activity and more daylight matter more than Sunday's traffic.
Take Columbia if you would rather pay $30 and skip I-95.**

## Three off-spec options worth knowing about

Each breaks one of your stated constraints, but each is the best at something.

### SAVANNAH, GA — best free evening, wrong position
**Sat 3:17 → arrive 13:50 · Sun 5:16 / 335 mi · detour +0:15 · 38% of the trip**

The richest free programme on the route, all on Nov 28: **Holiday Kickoff at Eastern Wharf,
17:00–21:00 — tree lighting, Santa, food trucks — plus the 25th Annual Lighted Boat Parade,
19:00–21:00, 30+ lit boats looping twice to the Talmadge Bridge. Both free.** Add the free
Savannah Belles Ferry (a real boat ride, to 22:00), the free Plant Riverside Christmas Market
and its free Generator Hall with a full-scale dinosaur replica. You also arrive at 13:50 with
3½ hours of daylight — the Mighty Eighth Air Force Museum in Pooler ($44, indoor, closes
17:00) is right off I-95.

**Why it is not the recommendation: at 38% of the trip it front-loads the easy day and leaves
335 miles of I-95 for Thanksgiving Sunday.** Stay here and you must be northbound before
10:00. Best hotel is **Drury Plaza Pooler** — indoor pool, free hot breakfast, **free 5:30
Kickback hot food and drinks**, free parking; dinner, breakfast and parking all cost nothing.
Or **Holiday Inn Express Hardeeville at I-95 Exit 8** (~$129, heated indoor pool), already
past the Talmadge Bridge, which rebalances Sunday a little.

### CHARLESTON, SC — best drive-through lights, double the detour
**Sat 5:00 → arrive 15:50 · Sun 4:41 / 292 mi · detour +1:22 · 52% of the trip**

The **best-balanced split on the whole route** (52%) and the best single light event:
**Holiday Festival of Lights**, James Island County Park, **17:30–22:00, $25/vehicle online**
($35 at the gate) — a 3-mile drive-through with 750+ displays and **unlimited loops on one
entry**, plus a village with a train, carousel, climbing wall, **free marshmallow fire pits**
and Santa. Enter from Folly Road via Griffith or Grimball, not the Central Park Rd route GPS
prefers. Also available: **Aquarium Aglow** with **Scuba Claus diving the Great Ocean Tank**
($109.80 at the 18:30 slot).

**The detour is +1:22 — nearly double your budget.** It is only clearly worth it if you spend
Sunday morning on the **USS Yorktown** at Patriots Point (10:00–12:30, $102), which is
plausibly the thing the boys would remember longest — at the cost of reaching Chapel Hill
around 18:00 on the worst traffic evening of the year. Note that **every indoor pool in the
Charleston metro is in North Charleston or Summerville** (Hilton's own filter returns zero for
Charleston and zero for Mount Pleasant), and downtown valet parking is **$34–62**, not the
$25–40 I assumed. Stay at **Embassy Suites Charleston Airport**: indoor heated pool, free hot
breakfast, two-room suites, $10 parking, sitting at the I-26/I-526 junction.

### SOUTHERN PINES, NC — best single event, abandons the halfway premise
**Sat 7:19 → arrive 18:09 · Sun 1:09 / 72 mi · detour +0:09 · 86% of the trip**

**Sandhills Garden of Lights** — inaugural year, **Nov 28 open 17:00–22:00**: 1M+ lights,
interactive light orbs, a ~0.4-mile flat walk-through, **Santa present Nov 27–29**, free
parking with shuttles. And the **Southern Pines tree lighting the same evening** — Santa by
fire truck 16:30, tree lit 18:15, free. Then Sunday is a **1:09 hop home**, with Weymouth
Woods available as a 45-minute morning stop.

**Why it is off-spec: 86% of the trip on Saturday, arriving 18:09.** That is a long day in
the car for a 6-year-old and not a halfway stop by any reading. **Two cautions:** Garden of
Lights prices are explicitly demand-based and **not published anywhere** — call
**910-695-3884** — and an inaugural year on Thanksgiving Saturday with timed entry is a
sell-out risk, so book now. The tree lighting date is sourced to three local blogs and is
**absent from the town's own calendar** — confirm it.

## What is not verified

Stated plainly, because three weeks out these are the things that could change the answer:

- **No live Nov 28 2026 hotel rate was obtainable for any property in any city.** Hilton,
  Marriott, Hyatt, IHG, Choice and Expedia all block automated access (403/429). Every price
  in these files is a published "from" rate, a directory baseline, or the GSA per diem —
  **not a quote.** Holiday-weekend surge pricing is unverified either way.
- **The exact Riverbanks Wild Lights price tier for Nov 28** — the portal returns 403. The
  $74–97 range is my arithmetic on the published band.
- **Sandhills Garden of Lights pricing** is not published anywhere (demand-based), and the
  **Southern Pines tree lighting** is blog-sourced and absent from the town's own calendar.
- **Orangeburg and Santee lodging** — no price evidence at all.
- **Airbnb/VRBO was never covered** — the session's 200-call web-search budget ran out first.
- **Fort Pulaski's Nov 28 hours** — open on the standing rule, but a 2023 release announced
  an early Thanksgiving close and no 2026 notice exists.
- **Thanksgiving traffic is not in any drive time here.** Valhalla has no traffic model;
  these are free-flow times and a lower bound. That understates the I-95 options more than
  it understates Columbia.
- **Weather normals were only obtained for the coast** (Charleston Nov 69°F/46°F, Hilton Head
  70°F/50°F). I could not verify Columbia's — the search budget was gone. Expect cooler
  inland, and dress for a 19:00 outdoor walk near 45°F.
